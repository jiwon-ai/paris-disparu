# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the stranger
[stranger_hello]
MAN WITH NOTEBOOK: Shh. I'm counting.

Three lorries. One cat. Two flat caps. One person in a raincoat.

Ah, you're the raincoat. Good evening. Or good morning. Hard to say at this hour.

I write down everything that passes in this street. Nobody else ever does.

That noise down there? Les Halles. Go and see. I'll note you when you're back.

And if you see something surprising, call me: 4127. I note calls too.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- the porter

[porter_ask]
FERNAND: So, are you helping or just watching?
? Yes | No

[porter_yes]
FERNAND: Good. Take them from my cart, one at a time. Look at what's inside.

And lift with your legs, not your back. A back has to last a lifetime.

[porter_no]
FERNAND: Suit yourself. The crates won't carry themselves, though.

[porter_working]
FERNAND: Cart's here, stalls are inside. Go on, go on!

[porter_done]
FERNAND: Three crates, three stalls. Not bad for a beginner.

I carry loads like that all night. By morning I can't feel my shoulders.

Go to the café on the street. Tell Simone that Fernand sent you.

[porter_after]
FERNAND: Still here? The café is on the street. Simone doesn't bite. Hardly.
