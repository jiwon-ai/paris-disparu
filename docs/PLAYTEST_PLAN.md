# Playtest plan (not yet run)

Goal: check that wandering is interesting without instructions. Observe
behaviour first; ask questions after.

## Setup
- 5-8 players who have not seen the game, on an emulator (or hardware when available).
- No instructions beyond the controls. Screen and hands recorded with consent.
- Observer takes timestamped notes; no help unless the player is stuck > 2 min.

## Measures (from observation)
| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Time to first interaction | first A press that opens text |
| Time to first meaningful discovery | first NPC conversation or telephone examination |
| Exploration | rooms visited, time per room, hotspots examined / available |
| Interaction rate | dialogues opened per minute |
| Confusion | moments of aimless back-and-forth > 30 s, questions asked aloud |
| Completion of A / C | crates delivered, telephone used (optional, not a success criterion) |
| Voluntary continuation | does the player keep playing after being told they may stop? |

Playtime alone is not treated as evidence of enjoyment.

## Questions after play
1. What was the most interesting moment?
2. Did you feel curious about exploring?
3. Did the place feel alive?
4. Did anything feel confusing or boring?
5. Would you keep playing on your own?
6. Did you notice something unexpected about Paris then?
Plus a 1-5 rating for enjoyment and for curiosity about the history.
