# Game review: a first-time player's view (2026-11-24)

What a new player meets, what holds them, and what was changed. Measured
against the gaps in docs/MARKET_ANALYSIS.md (branch docs/market-analysis):
music, density, save, polish.

## What already works

- **Hook in the first two minutes:** a place and a date on screen, a missing
  man, a cat to follow to the café, and an NPC who calls out what to do.
- **Clear goal:** the notebook (START) always shows the next step; Fernand
  and the man with the notebook say what is still missing.
- **A world that answers:** about 50 things to examine, 20+ people, five
  mini-games, nine secrets, a phone with four numbers to find.
- **A theme no one else has:** Les Halles the night before it vanished, with
  sources for every fact.

## Gaps a top studio would close

| Gap | Why it matters | Done in this pass |
|---|---|---|
| Few "what is this?" moments | Curiosity is what makes people talk about a game | **Curiosities**: three vanished objects found as "???", identified by the one person who knows (notebook page 4) |
| The night does not pass | No sense of progress through the world itself | **Clock 03:00 → 07:00** in the notebook, one hour per quest, the sky slightly bluer each hour |
| No reason to play again | Replay and comparing results drive word of mouth | **End rank** from everything found (26 things): Tourist, Passer-by, Night owl, True fort, Paris legend |
| Music and save | Market analysis: top gaps | Done earlier (7 original songs, battery save) |
