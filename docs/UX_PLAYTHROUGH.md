# First-time player walkthrough (2026-11-23)

A pass from the title screen as someone who has never seen the game,
listing every point where it was unclear what to do, and what was changed.

| # | Where | Unclear before | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Telephone | Only "Up/Down, then A": no way to know how to move between digits, or which number to call | Rules card the first time; the dial box shows "Up/Down: number", "Left/Right: digit", "A:call START:notes"; START fills in the next number written in the notebook |
| 2 | Telephone | Nothing says a jeton is needed or where to get one | New goal "Need a jeton? Win one: café porter." and a "!" over the porter |
| 3 | Every mini-game (lift, punch, coffee rush, cups, cabbages) | The rules and buttons were never stated | A "HOW TO PLAY" card before the first try of each: goal, buttons, "Missed? Try again." (A: start, B: back) |
| 4 | Anywhere | Who to talk to next | A "!" floats over the person the current goal points to |
| 5 | Anywhere | What can be looked at | An "A" bubble over the player when A would do something |
| 6 | Anywhere | Forgetting the goal | Standing still for 10 s shows the current goal for 4 s; the notebook (START) always shows it |
| 7 | Opening | "Look around." was vague | The "!" points at the man with the notebook |

Failing a mini-game never costs anything: the person asks again. This is said on the cards.
Not changed yet: a guaranteed-win practice round (the free retry covers it).
