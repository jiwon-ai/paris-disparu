# Historical research

Status labels:

- **Verified** — confirmed in a source listed here, which has actually been read.
- **Widely reported** — stated by several secondary sources; primary source still to be checked.
- **Reconstruction** — plausible invention for gameplay, not a historical claim.
- **Fiction** — invented characters and events.
- **To research** — unverified.

At this stage **no claim is marked Verified**: the primary archives named in
the project brief (BnF/Gallica, Archives de Paris, INA, Carnavalet, RATP) have
not yet been consulted.

## Game clock and dawn (2026-11-24)

The notebook shows 03:00 at the start and one hour later per quest done, up
to 07:00; the night palette turns slightly bluer each hour. The hours are
**game fiction** (pacing). They stay before daylight on purpose: Paris
sunrise on 23 Nov is about 08:11 CET (time.now sunrise page, read
2026-11-24; the same page gives an inconsistent civil-twilight time, so only
"after 8 a.m." is relied on).

Ending (added 2026-11-24): after Fernand's scene the game returns to the quai
and the night palettes fade to a pale morning, captioned "Tue 21 Nov 1967,
about 8 a.m., day breaks". The weekday is **computed** (21 Nov 1967 was a
Tuesday); "about 8 a.m." rests on the sunrise above (about 08:11 on 23 Nov, so
a few minutes earlier on 21 Nov: **inference**). France kept CET all year in
1967 (summer time came back in 1976: **inference from general knowledge, not
read for this project**). The jump from 07:00 to daybreak and the morning
colours are **game fiction / reconstruction**.
