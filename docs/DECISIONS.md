# Decisions and feedback log

What the creative director (Jiwon) asked for or decided, and what was done.
Newest first. Dates are 2026.

| Date | Feedback / decision | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| 11-24 | "Doesn't feel like a pro game yet: more fun" | Plan (in order): music, juice (fades, item-get, footsteps, shake), fill empty rooms, save/continue, a "vanished Paris" album. Step 1 done: 7 original loops (musette waltzes and polkas: title/end, street, market, café, river, métro, mini-games), VBL-driven driver, effects layered over music. Preview: a PC rendering of the same notes and an emulator recording of the title. Step 2 (juice): rooms fade out and in (3 steps each way), a gold star rises and blinks over the player for 1.6 s on every new clue, quest, extra or secret, and the screen shakes when the pickpocket bumps into the player. Step 3 (fuller rooms): market gets wicker baskets, more crates, a resting fort and a cook in a white apron; café gets bentwood chairs and a third table; métro hall gets posters (with a nod to the 1881 law), a bench and a night worker in blue work clothes. Each new thing is examinable or talks (EN/KO). Generic, no new historical claim. Bank 1 was full: `dlg_page_count` moved to bank 3 |
