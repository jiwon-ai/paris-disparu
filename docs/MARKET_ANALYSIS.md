## 2. Lessons that apply to a GBC exploration game

1. **Music is expected.** Deadeus added new music for its cartridge; Infinity
   sold a name-composer soundtrack as a stretch goal. Shovel Knight broke
   authenticity to keep music playing under SFX.
2. **Free first, paid later.** Deadeus was a free download (10,000+) before a
   $39.99 cart; demos are the most stable marketing effect; itch.io works for
   free games.
3. **Dense, curious spaces, not big empty ones.** "Gameplay per square inch";
   landmarks and collectables that pull the player; secrets implied by scenery.
4. **Short is fine if it is full.** A Short Hike: 1-2 h, praised for being
   full of surprises. Deadeus: 1-3 h per run with 11 endings.
5. **Replay value sells cartridges.** Deadeus lists replayability on the box;
   multiple endings are its pitch.
6. **Polish the text and fix bugs before the physical run.** Deadeus did a full
   text pass for the cart; playtest with strangers early (A Short Hike).
7. **Hardware matters on carts.** Infinity's 3.3 V/5 V problem cost a warranty
   extension and a board redesign.
8. **A clear pitch in three USPs, strong key art, short trailer, real
   screenshots.**
9. **Localisation widens reach.** Infinity localised into five languages.
10. **Modernise, do not copy.** Rose-tinted retro: keep the look, remove the
    pain (flicker, losing progress, unclear goals).

## 4. Prioritised improvements

**P0 — needed before anyone outside sees it**
1. **Original music**: a title theme and 3-4 loops (street, market, café, quai)
   plus an ending cue, with SFX layered over music. (Lesson 1)
2. **Browser-playable build + free demo on itch.io**: embed the ROM in a web
   Game Boy emulator so it runs in a browser tab, with on-screen/keyboard
   controls. Also what the user asked for ("브라우저로"). (Lesson 2)
3. **Fill the rooms**: shrink or furnish the empty floors (café tables and
   customers, métro posters/turnstiles/passengers, market crowds), more idle
   NPC animation, more scenery-implied secrets. (Lesson 3)
4. **Save / continue**: MBC5+RAM+BATTERY header and an SRAM save slot;
   required for a cartridge and for anything over 15 minutes. (Lessons 7, 10)
5. **Run the playtest plan** with 3-5 people new to the game; time the first
   play-through, log where they get stuck, then do a text pass. (Lesson 6)

**P1 — needed before selling**
6. **Length 45-90 min and more endings**: grow Episode 1 with one more area or
   quest line, and make endings depend on choices/extras (e.g. 4+ endings).
   (Lessons 4, 5)
7. **A "vanished Paris" album**: a collection screen of the period objects
   found (jeton, petit bleu, ticket, coltin…) with a one-line real fact each:
   a collection goal and the game's USP in one. (Lessons 3, 5) *(judgement)*
8. **Cuter, larger character sprites/portraits** to match the chibi brief and
   read well in thumbnails. (Lesson 8)
9. **Compatibility**: test in SameBoy, BGB, mGBA and on a real GBC/Chromatic;
   for carts, check every chip for 5 V. (Lesson 7)
10. **Legal review of the 7 cameos** (real people's likenesses) before any sale.

**P2 — release**
11. **Pitch**: three USPs, e.g. "Walk the Paris that vanished (Les Halles,
    1967)", "A gentle no-combat mystery", "Every historical detail sourced".
    Trailer < 90 s, annotated screenshots. (Lesson 8)
12. **French (then Korean/Japanese) localisation**; French is natural for the
    setting. (Lesson 9)
13. **Release path**: free web demo → paid ROM on itch.io → pitch a publisher
    (Incube8, ModRetro) for a cartridge. Deadeus's cart sold at $39.99; prices
    for paid digital GB ROMs were not found in these sources. (Lessons 2, 7)

## 6. Episode structure: Paris grows by métro (Jiwon's idea, 2026-11-24)

Each finished episode opens a new neighbourhood, reached from the métro
ticket hall that already exists in Episode 1. Why it fits the evidence above:

- Gradual unlocking: Shovel Knight opened levels "in sets of two then three"
  instead of all at once so players are not overwhelmed
  ([Kotaku 2018](https://kotaku.com/how-shovel-knights-developers-modernized-nes-game-desig-1829767941)).
- A growing world is a visible goal and a hook to the next episode, and each
  new area can carry its own "vanished Paris" objects for the album (P1 item 7).
  *(judgement)*

What it needs:

- **One ROM with save** (P0 item 4), so unlocked stations carry over. MBC5
  allows ROMs far larger than the current 128 KiB, so space is not the limit.
- A métro map screen as the hub: stations greyed out until their episode is
  finished.
- Each station and line must be checked for 1967 before it is used (stations
  that opened, closed or were renamed later); none are assumed here.
